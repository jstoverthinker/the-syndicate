## What this is

The Syndicate is an open-source collective focused on infrastructure that is actually maintained.

We work on:
- reclaiming abandoned dependencies,
- reviewing open-source supply-chain risk,
- deploying self-hosted tools,
- and building governance that rewards real work.

## Why this exists

A lot of software breaks quietly:
- dependencies get abandoned,
- maintainers burn out,
- security issues sit unresolved,
- and critical tools disappear without a replacement.

The Syndicate exists to reduce that damage.

## What we build

- **Reclaim** — recover and maintain abandoned or neglected software.
- **Review** — audit open-source supply-chain and dependency risk.
- **Works** — ship useful fixes, tools, and maintenance.
- **Deploy** — publish self-hosted infrastructure and practical tooling.
- **Continuity** — keep the work alive with process, records, and handoff paths.

## How it works

- Work is the stake.
- Contribution earns standing.
- Transparency matters.
- The goal is usable infrastructure, not slogans.
